# 🚀 AREON – Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

**Explore Mars. Survive the Mission. Discover the Unknown.**

AREON is an educational, first-person 3D Mars survival training experience designed for children aged **7–12 years**. Developed for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**, AREON transforms Mars exploration into an interactive learning adventure where players experience the challenges of surviving on the Red Planet.

Players explore the Martian environment, collect scientific data, manage limited resources, and make critical survival decisions while learning about real Mars conditions.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Mars is an extreme environment where survival requires careful planning and resource management.

AREON places players in **Arcadia Planitia, Mars**, where they undertake a multi-Sol exploration mission.

The objective is to collect three scientific data files across three Martian days (Sols), manage essential survival resources, and safely return to the habitat dome before nightfall.

Every action costs Power. Every decision matters.

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Mechanics

### 1. Resource Management
Players must monitor and manage essential survival resources:

- **Oxygen:** Maintain sufficient oxygen for survival.
- **Power:** Manage energy consumption during exploration.
- **Radiation Shield:** Monitor radiation protection.
- **Time:** Complete objectives and return before nightfall.

### 2. Scientific Exploration
- Explore the Martian terrain.
- Discover and collect three scientific data files.
- Complete mission objectives across three Sols.
- Experience an interactive Mars exploration environment.

### 3. Survival Simulation
- Experience resource consumption during gameplay.
- Understand the challenges of operating on Mars.
- Make decisions based on environmental conditions.
- Return safely to the habitat dome.

## 🔬 Real-World Science & NASA Data

AREON incorporates real Mars environmental science to make the experience educational and meaningful.

The project uses Mars weather and environmental information, including data associated with NASA's Curiosity Rover REMS (Rover Environmental Monitoring Station).

This helps connect gameplay with real scientific observations and introduces players to:

- Martian environmental conditions.
- Temperature variations.
- Radiation and survival challenges.
- The differences between Earth and Mars.
- The importance of resource management in space exploration.

**Our goal:** Make space science interactive, understandable, and engaging for young explorers.
